The platform

Turn scattered public sources into reviewable evidence.

Harvy connects collection, provenance, search, and reporting without hiding where information came from or what remains unknown.

01

Traceable collection

Keep source URLs, timestamps, collection methods, and known gaps alongside each observation.

02

Search and dossiers

Find collected evidence through research, query, and dossier interfaces built around the source record.

03

Layered storage

Use R2 for source artifacts and D1 for operational indexes; historical analytics are being wired through Basin.

What’s next

A roadmap with the status made clear.

Items marked in development are scaffolds or local implementations—not claims of a live deployment.

In use

Research and evidence workflows

Python-based collection, source artifacts, operational indexes, and search/dossier interfaces.

In development

Platform v2 orchestration

Separate supervisor, connector queues, durable workflows, event projection, public progress, and dataset manifests. The v2 workers are not deployed.

Built, gated

Source-specific connectors

SAM.gov API and a broad source catalog are present in the v2 scaffold. SAM.gov remains disabled pending its API key and a verified sample run; catalog candidates are not active feeds.

Planned

Validated portal and bulk adapters

Build reusable portal, directory, CMS, and feed adapters one source at a time, with access, licensing, schema, rate, and coverage checks before activation.

Planned

Large, resumable imports

Add bounded partition processing for bulk files and documents after storage, checkpoints, and reconciliation gates are ready.
